This family had simply outgrown the space. Between baby items and toddler items they were bursting at the seams, and there is only so much decluttering solves when a household genuinely needs more room. The townhome itself was small, single level, two bedrooms and two bathrooms, and it needed some updates. Nothing dramatic. It just had not been refreshed in a while.

The obvious approach with a home that needs updates is to either invest in fixing it up before listing, or price it low and brace for a long, quiet market while buyers weigh whether it is worth the hassle. We did neither.

## Honest price, honest property

We priced it as-is, reflecting the real condition rather than padding the number and hoping someone would negotiate their way down to fair value anyway. No staging, no repairs, no paid prep. The sellers did the basic tidying they could reasonably manage and nothing more. This seller's real need was speed and simplicity, with no bandwidth or budget for repairs before listing.

What made that work is scarcity. Single level townhomes do not come on the market often in this area. A buyer who specifically wants one level does not have many chances to find one, and when a rare layout hits the market, even one that needs work, the right buyer moves fast rather than waiting for something more polished, because there is no guarantee another one shows up soon. I built the marketing around that layout, since it would draw buyers regardless of cosmetic condition.

## Five days

We listed at $220,000 and it sold in five days at $210,000. A fair number for what the property needed, and a fast, clean exit for a family who needed to move on to a bigger space without sinking time or money into a home they were already leaving.

The lesson here is not about upgrades or negotiating tactics. It is about knowing what actually makes a property valuable in a specific market. A single level townhome does not need to be perfect to move fast in Rock Hill. It needs to exist, be priced honestly, and find the buyer who has been waiting for exactly that layout.
