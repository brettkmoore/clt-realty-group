I had been working with this family for a little over two years before this house came up. They were relocating from upstate New York to the Carolinas. Their child had chosen a college in the South, and after one campus visit, warmer weather started sounding good to the whole family. Over those two years the search went on and off. Some months they were actively looking, some months life got in the way. What kept the relationship alive was staying in touch about market conditions and what was happening in the Charlotte area, even in the stretches when there was nothing to show them.

Because they had taken the time to figure out exactly which locations they wanted, when the right house came up we both recognized it immediately. Gorgeous, meticulously maintained, the kind of curb appeal you do not have to talk anyone into. The one thing holding it back from perfect: the roof, the HVAC and the brass fixtures were all original.

## No showing, no problem

When they were in town to see homes, we could not get access to this one. Most buyers and most agents would call that a dead end. Instead I filmed a video walkthrough myself and sent it to them. Then I went back and shot more footage to answer every question they had. They fell in love with a house they had never physically walked through.

## Negotiating twice

We already knew the roof and HVAC were original, so we built that into the first offer, $635,000 against a $650,000 list, before an inspection ever happened. We ordered a full set of inspections to validate the condition of a home they were buying on video. When the report surfaced additional items, we went back to the table a second time. A lot of buyers treat "we already negotiated once" as the finish line. The inspection gave us real leverage and we used it. Final price: $625,000, $25,000 under the original list.

## After closing

From offer to closing took about two weeks. Then, from June through August, I checked on the vacant house every two weeks and coordinated contractor quotes for the roof and HVAC replacement while the family finished their move. That is not written into any standard contract. It is what turns a single transaction into a client for life.
